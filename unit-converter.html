FAQ

Is an M12 bolt the same as 1/2"?

No. 1/2" = 12.7 mm, so a 1/2" bolt is noticeably fatter than M12 (12.0 mm). They are only rough substitutes, and their threads never mesh — see the substitution table above with exact differences.

Why do my decimal inches not match the drill chart?

Machine shop charts use fractions (1/64" increments) and letter/number drill sizes. Use the fraction column above, or our tap drill chart which lists both.
